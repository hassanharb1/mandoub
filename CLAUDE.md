# دفتر المندوب (Mandoub) — project notes for Claude Code

A mobile PWA for a sales rep at an electrical-supplies company in Alexandria, Egypt. Built by **MESTK** (Hassan's company — always spelled MESTK, never MSETK). The rep is Hassan's friend; this is a live trial, with a SaaS version for many reps/companies as a possible next step.

Talk to Hassan in Egyptian Arabic. All UI copy is natural Egyptian Arabic: short, plain, no tashkeel.

## The user and his problem
- The rep visits traders and shops (عملاء) and takes orders. He carries **no stock**.
- He sends each order to a colleague at the company over WhatsApp. The colleague enters it into the company system, and the company ships to the customer.
- He **collects payments himself** and hands the cash to the company.
- Before the app he used phone notes plus WhatsApp. His pain points: forgetting to send orders (for example, an order taken on Tuesday that both he and the customer forgot), tracking collections, and remembering customer requests for items not in stock.
- His phone is **Android** with Chrome. Hassan tests on an iPhone.
- What the rep said matters most: **"the important thing is that it's easy to use on the phone."**

## What the app does (v1, in production)
- **النهارده (Home):** cash in hand, money still owed by customers, collected today, orders today, two buttons «طلب جديد» and «📝 النوتة», plus a «محتاج تفتكر» list (top 3 by urgency, «شوف الكل» shows the rest; `filt.homeAll`) and the last 4 orders as one-line rows (`orderRow`). That list shows unsent draft orders, sent orders nobody confirmed, customers waiting for something, customers who broke their usual ordering rhythm, and overdue balances.
- **العملاء (Customers):** list sorted by balance. «امسح العميل ده» in the customer form deletes him with his orders, requests, collections and account lines, after a second tap that says how many will go. A visit screen shows the balance, open orders, pending requests, what the customer usually buys, a "repeat last order" button and a ledger.
- **📣 رسالة واتساب لكذا عميل** (العملاء page, `bcSheet`/`bcRun`): pick a ready message (`S.settings.msgList [{id,name,text}]`, seeded by `bcMsgs()` from `BC_MSGS` «سلام / معدّي عليكم / بضاعة جديدة»; each has a short name and can be edited («احفظ التعديل»), saved as a new one («احفظها كرسالة جديدة») or deleted, all inside the sheet; last used in `S.settings.lastMsg`) where `{الاسم}` becomes the customer's name as registered (`c.name`), tick customers (quick picks: الكل, ماطلبوش من أسبوعين, ماكلّمتهمش من أسبوع, each area; customers without a phone can't be ticked), then send one by one: each «ابعت» opens `wa.me/<number>?text=…` (WhatsApp can't be sent automatically without the paid Business API), the rep taps send and comes back to the next one; «فوّته» skips. The queue `S.bcRun {text, ids, done, sent}` survives closing the app («كمّل الرسايل (فاضل N)»); `c.lastMsg` records the last message, shown on the customer page. Hassan said no «اللي عليهم فلوس» filter.
- **Search** everywhere (products, customers, picker) goes through `norm()`/`has()`: أ/إ/آ = ا, ة = ه, ى = ي, ؤ/ئ, tashkeel ignored, Arabic digits = 0-9.
- **Customer picker** (order, collection, quick capture): a search-as-you-type panel, empty query lists the 8 most recently touched customers (orders/payments/requests); the chosen id sits in a hidden input that fires `change`.
- **طلب جديد (New order):** pick a customer (خصم العميل % and الدفع sit right under it), then a «★ بياخد عادةً» chip with that customer's usual products (opened by default when he has history), then products by company tab (إيفورا / وايت لايت / «اخترت»), search (name or code, across both companies) and category chips. Each product row has its photo (tap to enlarge), price per piece, +/− with a **ق (قطعة) / ك (كرتونة)** toggle next to the count, and per line: **اللون** chips (`BODY`: ابيض/اسود/دهبي/ابيض في اسود → `item.color`), **الإضاءة** chips (`LIGHT`: ابيض/ورم → `item.light`) and a free note (`item.note`). **«+ لون تاني»** adds another line of the same product with its own count, ق/ك, colour, light and note (✕ removes it): the editor keeps `sel[pid] = {per, ls:[…]}` and refs lines as `pid:k`. Each line is its own order item; `itemTags(i)` shows «اللون: … · الإضاءة: …» in the message, picture, cards and sheet. A **«الحساب» card** like the bottom of the company invoice: **المجموع** from the price list (a ك line needs «الكرتونة فيها كام قطعة», remembered per product in `S.carton`, stored as `item.per`; lines without a price are counted in a warning), «تعديل» to type the sum by hand (`o.subManual`), **خصم العميل** as % (`o.disc`) or amount (`o.discAmt`, the other one is computed), and **الإجمالي بعد الخصم**; `orderMoney(o)` does the maths. The totals go into the message and the picture (dark totals box, line totals per item). «+ عميل جديد» under the customer picker adds a customer (name, area) without leaving the order. A free-text item is still possible. Each order also has **الدفع** كاش / آجل / بعدين (`o.pay`: `cash` / `credit` / `""`; changeable from the order sheet; a sent order still on «بعدين» shows in «محتاج تفتكر»). Both appear in the message and, in the picture, as a highlighted strip under the header. Save as a draft, or send.
- **Sending:** the send sheet draws the order as a **picture with the product photos** (canvas → JPEG) and the rep picks **«ابعت الصورة»** (picture only) **or «ابعت الرسالة»** (text only) — not both; each opens the share menu so it can go to the colleague or a WhatsApp group (text falls back to `wa.me`). Order status steps: draft → sent → registered → shipped → delivered. An edited order is still titled «طلب رقم N» (no «تعديل على»), as the rep asked.
- **المنتجات (Catalog tab):** browse both price lists as a photo grid, search, tap a product to enlarge it or start an order with it.
- **Adding and editing products:** «+ منتج» in المنتجات, «تعديل» in the product lightbox, and «ضيفه للقايمة» on a free-text item in the order editor (opens over the order as a stacked sheet, `sheet(...,{stack:true})` → `#scrim2`; `closeSheet` closes the top one). `productSheet(pid)` has a photo (camera/gallery → `shrinkPhoto`, ~360px WebP/JPEG), name, company chips (+ «شركة جديدة»), category (datalist), price per piece, code, pieces per carton. The rep's layer lives in `S.cat`: `add` (his products, ids `u_…`, tag «مضاف منك»), `edit[id]` (only the fields that differ from `catalog.js`, tag «معدّل», «رجّعه زي الأصل»; `hide:true` hides it, listed under «منتجات مخفية» to bring back) and `cos` (his companies, a tab appears once it has a product). `rebuildCat(S.cat)` merges `BASE` (catalog.js) with it into `CAT`/`PMAP`/`HIDDEN`/`ALLCOS`; call it after any change. His photos are in IndexedDB `mandoub-img` (key → data URL, product `img:"u:key"`), read into `UIMG` on open; backups carry them as `_img` and restore writes them back. Clearing or restoring the demo keeps `S.cat`. Old orders keep their name/price snapshot.
- **سجّل بسرعة (Quick capture):** customer plus free text (or voice), saved as a pending request until it is converted to an order or closed.
- **الفلوس (Money):** record collections, record «سلّمت للشركة» handovers to the company, cash in hand = collections − handovers, money owed, and recent movements. Collections and handovers have a **date (day picker, weekday shown)** and can be **edited or deleted** by tapping them (in الفلوس or the customer's ledger). «سجّل وابعته للجروب» saves a collection and opens the share menu with a short text (customer, amount, day, remaining balance) so it can go to a WhatsApp group or a person.
- **تحصيلات يوم (Money):** pick a day (today by default): each collection with customer and note (e.g. «الأسبوع اللي فات» for an old debt), the total, and «ابعت صورة» (canvas picture) / «ابعت رسالة» to share with the colleague. «📝 النوتة» (`noteSheet`, also on Home) is a free-text note: one collection per line («محمد 500», «سيد 2 ألف قديمة», «1- النور: 1,200 ج تحويل»). `parseNoteLine` reads name, amount (Arabic digits, commas, «ألف/آلاف/ألفين», ج/جنيه dropped) and note; a live preview shows each line matched to a customer (`matchCust`: exact normalised name ✓, several close ones → «تقصد مين؟» chips, none → «عميل جديد», created on save); a matched customer who owes shows «عليه X ← Y» (running across lines, red when it goes below zero), errors for lines without a name or amount, and the total. The day the money came in (`p.date`) is at the top; a line saying «قديمة» or «اللي فات» gets a «دفعة قديمة عن يوم» date (`p.forDate`, default a week back; «دفعة قديمة» in the single collection sheet too). The text is kept as a draft (`S.noteDraft {t, day}`) until saved. After saving, `noteDone` lists the new collections with «صورة»/«رسالة» each and «ابعت الكل صورة/رسالة» (`dayColImage/dayColText(iso, list)`). The day list, text and picture split «دفعات اليوم» / «دفعات قديمة (عن يوم …)» with subtotals. Each row in the day list also has «صورة» / «رسالة» to send just that one collection (a receipt picture, `payImage`), and the edit-collection sheet starts with the same two buttons.
- **Home collections counter:** «حصّلت النهارده» counts from `S.colFrom` (start of today if never set). Tapping it lists those collections (editable) and «صفّر العداد» sets `S.colFrom` to now — nothing is deleted and cash in hand doesn't change.
- **رصيد أول المدة (opening balance):** flexible — عليه (owes) or ليه (credit, stored negative), with a «لحد يوم» date (`openingDate`); editable from the customer form or by tapping it in the ledger.
- **Notifications (no server):** on every save the page writes `notify.json` to the `mandoub-notify` cache: reminders for the 6 «محتاج تفتكر» kinds (draft not sent, sent not confirmed, customer waiting, late customer, overdue balance, no backup), each with the time it becomes due and switchable in Settings (`S.settings.notif`). `sw.js` shows the due ones on `periodicsync` (Chrome Android, installed app only, timing chosen by Chrome, roughly once or twice a day) and won't repeat the same list within 20 h. Not on iOS. Reliable, specific pushes need the sync server or the APK.
- **Updates:** `VERSION` in `index.html` must equal N in `sw.js`'s `CACHE "mandoub-vN"`. Settings → «تحديث البرنامج» shows the version and checks `sw.js?check=…` on the server; «حدّث دلوقتي» clears the app caches (not `mandoub-notify`, never the data) and reloads. On open, a bar offers the update when the server is ahead. `sw.js` fetches pages with `cache:"no-cache"` (and installs with `cache:"reload"`) so Chrome's HTTP cache never serves an old file; when a slow line made it open the saved copy and the fresh page arrives later, it posts `{type:"fresh"}` and the page offers «النسخة الجديدة نزلت». «حدّث دلوقتي» refuses to clear caches when the server can't be reached. The rep should never need to clear Chrome's cache.
- **Trial copy with activation codes:** the same `public` folder is also deployed as the worker `mandoub-trial` (`wrangler.trial.jsonc`). On a hostname containing "trial" (or with `localStorage["mandoub.forceTrial"]="1"` for testing) the app asks for a code (`XXXX-XXXX`) before it opens. `public/licenses.json` lists `{name, until, off, h}` per code, where `h` = SHA-256 of `"mandoub|"+code` without the dash (the plain codes are never in the repo; Hassan keeps them). The app re-checks on every open with internet (and after 6 h in the background) via `licenses.json?check=…` (bypasses the SW). It locks («التجربة خلصت», «الكود ده اتوقف», «افتح النت مرة») past `until`, when `off` is true, or after 3 days without reaching the server. The lock screen offers «جرّب تاني», a data backup and «كود تاني»; data is never touched. The licence lives in `localStorage["mandoub.lic"]`, outside `S`. To stop or extend a code: edit `licenses.json`, redeploy the trial worker. New codes are generated with a hash in the same format. The main site (the friend rep) has no code.
- **Settings:** rep name, colleague name and WhatsApp number, a reminder delay for unsent orders, backup and restore as JSON, clearing the demo data, restoring the demo data.
- **Commission was removed on purpose.** Hassan asked for that. Do not add it back.

## Tech
- One self-contained file, `public/index.html`, written in vanilla JS with no framework and no build step, plus `catalog.js` (the product list, ~30 KB, loaded before the app), `catalog-img.js` (all product photos as WebP data URIs, ~850 KB, loaded **after** the app opens so a slow line never blocks it; no image folder, so a dashboard upload can't miss it), `sw.js` (offline cache), `manifest.json` and the icons.
- Data lives in **localStorage** under the key `mandoub.v1`, as one JSON state object `S`: `settings, products, carton (unused now), customers, orders, requests, payments, handovers, adjusts, seq, demo, since, lastBackup, colFrom, noteDraft, cat, bcRun`. Products are **not** in `S`: they come from `catalog.js` (`self.CATALOG`); `S.products` only keeps products from older versions so old orders still resolve. Order items: `{pid, qty, unit:"ق"|"ك", color, light, note, price, name}` (the same pid can appear on several lines) (name and price are a snapshot).
- A customer's balance = `opening` + `S.adjusts` − payments. Orders are **not** added (no reliable amounts, and the rep never sees the invoice: the colleague deals with the company). The rep controls the account with **«ظبط الحساب»** on the customer page: «عليه كام» (set to the company's figure; the difference is stored), «زوّد عليه» or «نزّل من عليه». Each is a dated, editable line `{id, customerId, amount (signed), date, note}` in the ledger.
- **Products:** 228 real products from two company price lists Hassan sent (Aug 2026): **إيفورا** (Evora, 12-page scanned PDF, hand-transcribed; prices exclude VAT) and **وايت لايت** (White Light, 9-page table). Prices are per piece. Photos were cropped from the PDFs, ~320px WebP in `catalog-img.js`. To update prices, edit `catalog.js` directly; for a new list, re-extract.
- The app ships with **demo data** (fictional customers and orders, marked by a banner) that uses real catalog products. On boot, an old demo state (placeholder products `p1…p16`) is replaced by the new demo, keeping settings; non-demo data is never touched.
- **Never block the first paint** (the rep's Android sat on the splash screen once): the Google Font loads non-blocking, photos load after render, and `sw.js` falls back to the cached copy after 4 s on a slow line. A red banner shows any JS error on screen so the rep can screenshot it.
- On boot the app calls `navigator.storage.persist()`. A backup-reminder card appears on Home after 3 days without a backup, but only when demo mode is off.
- **Voice:** the Web Speech API (`ar-EG`) is used on Android only, with an 8 s no-speech watchdog and a 25 s hard stop. On **iOS the mic button is hidden** and a hint points to the keyboard's own mic, because Web Speech hangs in iOS home-screen web apps.
- **Wide screens (≥980px, laptop/desktop):** one `@media` block turns the bottom nav into a right sidebar with the brand, widens content to 1180px, lays Home/Customer/Money/Orders out in two columns (`main[data-view]`), shows the catalog in a 4–5 column grid, centres sheets as dialogs, and splits the order editor into two panes (`.oe-side` details + running order list `#opicked` | `.oe-main` products in two columns). On the phone those wrappers are `display:contents`, so the mobile layout is unchanged. Each device still has its own data until sync exists.
- Design: MESTK brand colors (Charcoal `#282825`, Burnt Orange `#BD572F`, Warm Cream `#F5F0E7`), the Cairo font, RTL, light and dark themes through CSS tokens. Body text is about 15px, tap targets are at least 36px, and the side gutter is 16px.

## Hosting and deploy
- **Live URL:** https://mandoub-mestk.hassan-mestkawy.workers.dev, served as a Cloudflare Worker with static assets. Hassan's Cloudflare account also hosts easy-drive-system and mestk-platform.
- **Netlify is not an option:** `*.netlify.app` is blocked in Egypt (ERR_CONNECTION_TIMED_OUT without a VPN).
- Deploy from this folder with `npx wrangler deploy` (it reads `wrangler.jsonc`, the worker name `mandoub-mestk` and the `./public` folder). The first run asks Hassan to log in with `npx wrangler login`. A manual alternative: Cloudflare dashboard → mandoub-mestk → New deployment → upload the `public` folder.
- **On every release, bump `CACHE` in `public/sw.js` and `VERSION` in `index.html` together** (currently `mandoub-v31`) so phones pick up the update. `sw.js` is network-first for the page, so the update arrives on the next app open. The user's data is never touched by a deploy.

## How to test
- `npx serve public` or `python -m http.server -d public`, then open it at a phone width (about 360–400px) in DevTools device mode.
- Check both themes, the Arabic text and numbers, no horizontal scroll, and the flow "new order → WhatsApp message → mark sent".
- To test the backup reminder: set `S.demo=false` and move `S.since` back more than 3 days in localStorage.
