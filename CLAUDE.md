# SalesGo by MESTK (formerly «دفتر المندوب», repo/worker «mandoub») — project notes for Claude Code

The app is branded **SalesGo** (Arabic pronunciation سيلز جو), signed **by MESTK**; Hassan chose SalesGo alone, without «دفتر المندوب» under it. A mobile PWA for a sales rep at an electrical-supplies company in Alexandria, Egypt. Built by **MESTK** (Hassan's company — always spelled MESTK, never MSETK). The rep is Hassan's friend; this is a live trial, with a SaaS version for many reps/companies as a possible next step.

Talk to Hassan in Egyptian Arabic. All UI copy is natural Egyptian Arabic: short, plain, no tashkeel.

## The user and his problem
- The rep visits traders and shops (عملاء) and takes orders. He carries **no stock**.
- He sends each order to a colleague at the company over WhatsApp. The colleague enters it into the company system, and the company ships to the customer.
- He **collects payments himself** and hands the cash to the company.
- Before the app he used phone notes plus WhatsApp. His pain points: forgetting to send orders (for example, an order taken on Tuesday that both he and the customer forgot), tracking collections, and remembering customer requests for items not in stock.
- His phone is **Android** with Chrome. Hassan tests on an iPhone.
- What the rep said matters most: **"the important thing is that it's easy to use on the phone."**

## What the app does (v1, in production)
- **النهارده (Home, like `brand/Home-light.png`):** title «النهارده» + date, the brand symbol tile, a «الفلوس اللي معاك» card (42px figure, «جنيه», when the last handover was), two cards «عند العملاء» / «تحصيل النهارده» (tap → the collections counter), buttons «طلب جديد» and «سجّل تحصيل» (opens النوتة), a «محتاج تفتكر» list of reminder cards (top 3 by urgency, «الكل» shows the rest; `filt.homeAll`; each has a worded tag such as «ما اتبعتش» / «مستني تأكيد», never colour alone) and the last 4 orders as one-line rows (`orderRow`). The bottom nav keeps «الطلبات» (Hassan's choice over the design's «النوتة»). That list shows unsent draft orders, sent orders nobody confirmed, customers waiting for something, customers who broke their usual ordering rhythm, and overdue balances.
- **العملاء (Customers):** list sorted by balance. «امسح العميل ده» in the customer form deletes him with his orders, requests, collections and account lines, after a second tap that says how many will go. A visit screen shows the balance, open orders, pending requests, what the customer usually buys, a "repeat last order" button and a ledger.
- **📣 رسالة واتساب لكذا عميل** (العملاء page, `bcSheet`/`bcRun`): pick a ready message (`S.settings.msgList [{id,name,text}]`, seeded by `bcMsgs()` from `BC_MSGS` «سلام / معدّي عليكم / بضاعة جديدة»; each has a short name and can be edited («احفظ التعديل»), saved as a new one («احفظها كرسالة جديدة») or deleted, all inside the sheet; last used in `S.settings.lastMsg`) where `{الاسم}` becomes the customer's name as registered (`c.name`), tick customers (quick picks: الكل, ماطلبوش من أسبوعين, ماكلّمتهمش من أسبوع, each area; customers without a phone can't be ticked), then send one by one: each «ابعت» opens `wa.me/<number>?text=…` (WhatsApp can't be sent automatically without the paid Business API), the rep taps send and comes back to the next one; «فوّته» skips. The queue `S.bcRun {text, ids, done, sent}` survives closing the app («كمّل الرسايل (فاضل N)»); `c.lastMsg` records the last message, shown on the customer page. Hassan said no «اللي عليهم فلوس» filter.
- **Search** everywhere (products, customers, picker) goes through `norm()`/`has()`: أ/إ/آ = ا, ة = ه, ى = ي, ؤ/ئ, tashkeel ignored, Arabic digits = 0-9.
- **Customer picker** (order, collection, quick capture): a search-as-you-type panel, empty query lists the 8 most recently touched customers (orders/payments/requests); the chosen id sits in a hidden input that fires `change`.
- **طلب جديد (New order, like `brand/New-order-light.png`):** a full-screen sheet with a back arrow and «حفظ مسودة» at the top (`sheet(...,{full,act})`); the customer is a labelled field; search, then chips «الكل / إيفورا / وايت لايت / اخترت N» (co "" = all companies) and category chips («كل الأقسام»). Products are cards: unselected ones show «+ ضيف»; adding opens the card (one open at a time, `openId`): كرتونة/قطعة, «الكمية» stepper, «لون: …» / «إضاءة: …» buttons that open their options underneath (`chooser`), «+ ملحوظة», pieces per carton, «+ لون تاني», «تمام»; other selected products collapse to a summary with «تعديل». A summary is fixed at the bottom («الإجمالي بعد خصم X%», «تعديل الخصم» scrolls to the «الحساب» card) with «مراجعة الطلب وإرساله» (save → the send sheet). The rep's word «ورم» stays (not «دافئة»). Older notes: pick a customer (خصم العميل % and الدفع sit right under it), then a «★ بياخد عادةً» chip with that customer's usual products (opened by default when he has history), then products by company tab (إيفورا / وايت لايت / «اخترت»), search (name or code, across both companies) and category chips. Each product row has its photo (tap to enlarge), price per piece, +/− with a **ق (قطعة) / ك (كرتونة)** toggle next to the count, and per line: **اللون** chips (`BODY`: ابيض/اسود/دهبي/ابيض في اسود → `item.color`), **الإضاءة** chips (`LIGHT`: ابيض/ورم → `item.light`) and a free note (`item.note`). **«+ لون تاني»** adds another line of the same product with its own count, ق/ك, colour, light and note (✕ removes it): the editor keeps `sel[pid] = {per, ls:[…]}` and refs lines as `pid:k`. When the product has its own options (`p.colors` / `p.lights`, ticked in the product sheet), a line offers only those, and no light row when it has no lights; a product with none ticked offers `BODY`/`LIGHT`. Each line is its own order item; `itemTags(i)` shows «اللون: … · الإضاءة: …» in the message, picture, cards and sheet. A **«الحساب» card** like the bottom of the company invoice: **المجموع** from the price list (a ك line needs «الكرتونة فيها كام قطعة», remembered per product in `S.carton`, stored as `item.per`; lines without a price are counted in a warning), «تعديل» to type the sum by hand (`o.subManual`), **خصم العميل** as % (`o.disc`) or amount (`o.discAmt`, the other one is computed), and **الإجمالي بعد الخصم**; `orderMoney(o)` does the maths. The totals go into the message and the picture (dark totals box, line totals per item). «+ عميل جديد» under the customer picker adds a customer (name, area) without leaving the order. A free-text item is still possible. Each order also has **الدفع** كاش / آجل / بعدين (`o.pay`: `cash` / `credit` / `""`; changeable from the order sheet; a sent order still on «بعدين» shows in «محتاج تفتكر»). Both appear in the message and, in the picture, as a highlighted strip under the header. Save as a draft, or send.
- **Sending:** the send sheet draws the order as a **picture with the product photos** (canvas → JPEG) and the rep picks **«ابعت الصورة»** (picture only) **or «ابعت الرسالة»** (text only) — not both; each opens the share menu so it can go to the colleague or a WhatsApp group (text falls back to `wa.me`). Order status steps: draft → sent → registered → shipped → delivered. An edited order is still titled «طلب رقم N» (no «تعديل على»), as the rep asked. Any order already sent has **«📤 ابعته تاني»** in its sheet: the same send sheet («ابعت الطلب تاني»), whose share menu reaches the colleague, a group or the customer; **«ابعته للعميل»** (shown whenever the customer has a phone) opens the customer's own chat via `wa.me` with the full message (prices and all, as Hassan asked). Sending again never changes the order's step.
- **المنتجات (Catalog tab):** browse both price lists as a photo grid, search, tap a product to enlarge it or start an order with it.
- **Adding and editing products:** «+ منتج» in المنتجات, «تعديل» in the product lightbox, and «ضيفه للقايمة» on a free-text item in the order editor (opens over the order as a stacked sheet, `sheet(...,{stack:true})` → `#scrim2`; `closeSheet` closes the top one). `productSheet(pid)` has a photo (camera/gallery → `shrinkPhoto`, ~360px WebP/JPEG), name, company chips (+ «شركة جديدة»), category (datalist), price per piece, code, pieces per carton, and **الألوان المتاحة / الإضاءة المتاحة** (multi-select chips from `BODY`/`LIGHT` plus «+ لون تاني» / «+ إضاءة تانية» for any other; saved as `colors`/`lights` only when something is ticked; Hassan said the app must not fill them from the product name). The rep's layer lives in `S.cat`: `add` (his products, ids `u_…`, tag «مضاف منك»), `edit[id]` (only the fields that differ from `catalog.js`, tag «معدّل», «رجّعه زي الأصل»; `hide:true` hides it, listed under «منتجات مخفية» to bring back) and `cos` (his companies, a tab appears once it has a product). `rebuildCat(S.cat)` merges `BASE` (catalog.js) with it into `CAT`/`PMAP`/`HIDDEN`/`ALLCOS`; call it after any change. His photos are in IndexedDB `mandoub-img` (key → data URL, product `img:"u:key"`), read into `UIMG` on open; backups carry them as `_img` and restore writes them back. Clearing or restoring the demo keeps `S.cat`. Old orders keep their name/price snapshot.
- **سجّل بسرعة (Quick capture):** customer plus free text (or voice), saved as a pending request until it is converted to an order or closed.
- **الفلوس (Money):** record collections, record «سلّمت للشركة» handovers to the company, cash in hand = collections − handovers, money owed, and recent movements. Collections and handovers have a **date (day picker, weekday shown)** and can be **edited or deleted** by tapping them (in الفلوس or the customer's ledger). «سجّل وابعته للجروب» saves a collection and opens the share menu with a short text (customer, amount, day, remaining balance) so it can go to a WhatsApp group or a person.
- **تحصيلات يوم (Money):** pick with the chips «النهارده» / «امبارح» (each with its count) / «من آخر تسليم» (everything collected after the last «سلّمت للشركة») / «يوم تاني» (`dcPickSheet`: من يوم … لحد يوم; same day twice = one day); today by default. Several days (`filt.dcR`, `dcRange()`) list one row per day (count + total, tap → that day) to keep it uncluttered, with the grand total and «ابعت صورة/رسالة» (`rangeColText`, `dayColImage(iso,list,rangeHead(R))`, grouped by day). The chips replaced a small «غيّر اليوم» link the rep never noticed (he thought sending a whole day was gone). One day shows each collection with customer and note (e.g. «الأسبوع اللي فات» for an old debt), the total, and «ابعت صورة» (canvas picture) / «ابعت رسالة» to share with the colleague. «📝 النوتة» (`noteSheet`, also on Home) is a free-text note: one collection per line («محمد 500», «سيد 2 ألف قديمة», «1- النور: 1,200 ج تحويل»). `parseNoteLine` reads name, amount (Arabic digits, commas, «ألف/آلاف/ألفين», ج/جنيه dropped) and note; a live preview shows each line matched to a customer (`matchCust`: exact normalised name ✓, several close ones → «تقصد مين؟» chips, none → «عميل جديد», created on save); a matched customer who owes shows «عليه X ← Y» (running across lines, red when it goes below zero), errors for lines without a name or amount, and the total. The day the money came in (`p.date`) is at the top; a line saying «قديمة» or «اللي فات» gets a «دفعة قديمة عن يوم» date (`p.forDate`, default a week back; «دفعة قديمة» in the single collection sheet too). The text is kept as a draft (`S.noteDraft {t, day}`) until saved. After saving, `noteDone` lists the new collections with «صورة»/«رسالة» each and «ابعت الكل صورة/رسالة» (`dayColImage/dayColText(iso, list)`). The day list, text and picture split «دفعات اليوم» / «دفعات قديمة (عن يوم …)» with subtotals. Each row in the day list also has «صورة» / «رسالة» to send just that one collection (a receipt picture, `payImage`), and the edit-collection sheet starts with the same two buttons.
- **Home collections counter:** «حصّلت النهارده» counts from `S.colFrom` (start of today if never set). Tapping it lists those collections (editable) and «صفّر العداد» sets `S.colFrom` to now — nothing is deleted and cash in hand doesn't change.
- **رصيد أول المدة (opening balance):** flexible — عليه (owes) or ليه (credit, stored negative), with a «لحد يوم» date (`openingDate`); editable from the customer form or by tapping it in the ledger.
- **Notifications (no server):** on every save the page writes `notify.json` to the `mandoub-notify` cache: reminders for the 6 «محتاج تفتكر» kinds (draft not sent, sent not confirmed, customer waiting, late customer, overdue balance, no backup), each with the time it becomes due and switchable in Settings (`S.settings.notif`). `sw.js` shows the due ones on `periodicsync` (Chrome Android, installed app only, timing chosen by Chrome, roughly once or twice a day) and won't repeat the same list within 20 h. Not on iOS. Reliable, specific pushes need the sync server or the APK.
- **Updates:** `VERSION` in `index.html` must equal N in `sw.js`'s `CACHE "mandoub-vN"`. Settings → «تحديث البرنامج» shows the version and checks `sw.js?check=…` on the server; «حدّث دلوقتي» clears the app caches (not `mandoub-notify`, never the data) and reloads. On open, a bar offers the update when the server is ahead. `sw.js` fetches pages with `cache:"no-cache"` (and installs with `cache:"reload"`) so Chrome's HTTP cache never serves an old file; when a slow line made it open the saved copy and the fresh page arrives later, it posts `{type:"fresh"}` and the page offers «النسخة الجديدة نزلت». «حدّث دلوقتي» refuses to clear caches when the server can't be reached. The rep should never need to clear Chrome's cache.
- **Trial copy with activation codes:** the same `public` folder is also deployed as the worker `mandoub-trial` (`wrangler.trial.jsonc`). On a hostname containing "trial" (or with `localStorage["mandoub.forceTrial"]="1"` for testing) the app asks for a code (`XXXX-XXXX`) before it opens. `public/licenses.json` lists `{name, until, off, h}` per code, where `h` = SHA-256 of `"mandoub|"+code` without the dash (the plain codes are never in the repo; Hassan keeps them). The app re-checks on every open with internet (and after 6 h in the background) via `licenses.json?check=…` (bypasses the SW). It locks («التجربة خلصت», «الكود ده اتوقف», «افتح النت مرة») past `until`, when `off` is true, or after 3 days without reaching the server. The lock screen offers «جرّب تاني», a data backup and «كود تاني»; data is never touched. The licence lives in `localStorage["mandoub.lic"]`, outside `S`. To stop or extend a code: edit `licenses.json`, redeploy the trial worker. New codes are generated with a hash in the same format. The main site (the friend rep) has no code.
- **Settings:** rep name, colleague name and WhatsApp number, a reminder delay for unsent orders, backup and restore as JSON, clearing the demo data, restoring the demo data.
- **Commission was removed on purpose.** Hassan asked for that. Do not add it back.

## Tech
- One self-contained file, `public/index.html`, written in vanilla JS with no framework and no build step, plus `catalog.js` (the product list, ~30 KB, loaded before the app), `catalog-img.js` (all product photos as WebP data URIs, ~850 KB, loaded **after** the app opens so a slow line never blocks it; no image folder, so a dashboard upload can't miss it), `sw.js` (offline cache), `manifest.json` and the icons.
- Data lives in **localStorage** under the key `mandoub.v1`, as one JSON state object `S`: `settings, products, carton (unused now), customers, orders, requests, payments, handovers, adjusts, seq, demo, since, lastBackup, colFrom, noteDraft, cat, bcRun`. Products are **not** in `S`: they come from `catalog.js` (`self.CATALOG`); `S.products` only keeps products from older versions so old orders still resolve. Order items: `{pid, qty, unit:"ق"|"ك", color, light, note, price, name}` (the same pid can appear on several lines) (name and price are a snapshot).
- A customer's balance = `opening` + **sent orders** + `S.adjusts` − payments. **Since v45 orders count** (Hassan approved; it was the main reason the rep's figures didn't match the accounts manager): every order not in «draft» whose send time (`ordAt(o)` = `o.hist.sent` or `created`) is on or after `S.ordFrom` adds its net after discount (`total(o)`). `S.ordFrom` is set to the moment v45 first opens, so older orders (already settled by hand) aren't counted twice; it syncs in `meta` (earliest wins). The ledger shows «طلب رقم N» lines (tap → the order; «من غير مبلغ» when it has no prices), `ledgerSheet` has «كل الحركات / الدفعات / الطلبات» and a «طلبات اتبعتت (N)» line, and the order sheet says whether it is on the account. **Customer statement (v50):** «كشف حساب للعميل (صورة أو رسالة)» on the customer page and at the bottom of `ledgerSheet` opens `stmtSheet`: chips «آخر شهر / آخر 3 شهور / كل الحساب» (`stmtData(cid, days)`: lines oldest first from `custLedger`, each with the running balance; what he owed before the period opens it as «رصيد سابق», or «رصيد أول المدة» for the whole account; more than 60 lines fold into the carried balance; the end always equals `balance()`), a picture (`stmtImage`: dark header with the symbol, «كشف حساب», name, period · area, rows date+description | amount (+ red / − green, drawn LTR) | balance, a final box «عليه دلوقتي» orange or «ليه عندنا» green, SalesGo · rep / by MESTK) and the text (`stmtText`). «ابعت الصورة» = share menu; «ابعت رسالة للعميل» opens his own WhatsApp chat (`wa.me/<phone>`) when he has a number, else the share menu. The rep controls the account with **«ظبط الحساب»** on the customer page: «عليه كام» (set to the company's figure; the difference is stored), «زوّد عليه» or «نزّل من عليه». Each is a dated, editable line `{id, customerId, amount (signed), date, note}` in the ledger.
- **Products:** 228 real products from two company price lists Hassan sent (Aug 2026): **إيفورا** (Evora, 12-page scanned PDF, hand-transcribed; prices exclude VAT) and **وايت لايت** (White Light, 9-page table). Prices are per piece. Photos were cropped from the PDFs, ~320px WebP in `catalog-img.js`. To update prices, edit `catalog.js` directly; for a new list, re-extract.
- The app ships with **demo data** (fictional customers and orders, marked by a banner) that uses real catalog products. On boot, an old demo state (placeholder products `p1…p16`) is replaced by the new demo, keeping settings; non-demo data is never touched.
- **Never block the first paint** (the rep's Android sat on the splash screen once): the Google Font loads non-blocking, photos load after render, and `sw.js` falls back to the cached copy after 4 s on a slow line. A red banner shows any JS error on screen so the rep can screenshot it.
- On boot the app calls `navigator.storage.persist()`. A backup-reminder card appears on Home after 3 days without a backup, but only when demo mode is off.
- **Voice:** the Web Speech API (`ar-EG`) is used on Android only, with an 8 s no-speech watchdog and a 25 s hard stop. On **iOS the mic button is hidden** and a hint points to the keyboard's own mic, because Web Speech hangs in iOS home-screen web apps.
- **Wide screens (≥980px, laptop/desktop):** one `@media` block turns the bottom nav into a right sidebar with the brand, widens content to 1180px, lays Home/Customer/Money/Orders out in two columns (`main[data-view]`), shows the catalog in a 4–5 column grid, centres sheets as dialogs, and splits the order editor into two panes (`.oe-side` details + running order list `#opicked` | `.oe-main` products in two columns). On the phone those wrappers are `display:contents`, so the mobile layout is unchanged. Each device still has its own data until sync exists.
- **Brand (SalesGo identity kit, Oct 2026; tokens and reference screens in `brand/`):** CSS tokens from `brand/design-tokens.css` (light: bg #F5F0E7, surface #FFF, raised #EDE5D9, text #282825, muted #65615A, border #93887A, divider #DED5C8, brand #BD572F, action #A74724 on #FFF; dark: bg #1C1C1A, surface #282825, raised #34342F, text #F5F0E7, muted #C6BDB0, action #BD572F on #FFF, brand text #E07B52 (Hassan found #F29A72 too loud; the dark logos use #BD572F too, and the nav badge uses the action colour); success/warning/error/info + their backgrounds). The old variable names (`--fg`, `--accent`, `--line`, `--ok`…) are aliases of these, so keep using either. Cairo 400–800; screen title 26, section 20, body 16, caption 14, hero money 42, card money 26. Spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32; radius card 16, button 12, chip 10; button 52 high, field 48, chip/tap target ≥44. Numbers are `.num` (LTR, isolated, tabular + lining figures) with «جنيه» apart. Icons are the kit's 24px 2px-stroke line icons (`ICON`/`icon(name)`). Logo assets: `logo-light/dark.svg` (horizontal, viewBox cropped), `logo-stacked-light/dark.svg` (splash), `symbol.svg`; app icons from the kit (`icon-192/512.png`, `icon-maskable(-192).png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` 180, `favicon.ico`, `icon-32.png`). The splash (`#splash`) is plain HTML that paints at once and is removed 450 ms after the first render. The worker and URL stay `mandoub-mestk` because the rep's data lives on that origin. Buttons are only primary (`.btn.primary`, action colour) or secondary (`.btn.ghost`; the old `.btn.dark` now looks the same); WhatsApp actions stay green (`--wa`). Signed amounts use `smoney(sign,n)` so «+2,000» reads as one number; quantities show as «40 قطعة». The customer page has a back arrow (`#hdrBack`). The WhatsApp pictures (order, day collections, receipt) carry the symbol in the header corner (`drawSymbol`) and «SalesGo» / «by MESTK» in the footer. Side gutter 16px, RTL.

The user's data is never touched by a deploy.

## Accounts and sync (v42, Cloudflare — Hassan chose it over Supabase)
- **Server:** the same worker now has a script, `worker/index.js` (`main` in both wrangler files, assets bound as `ASSETS`): `/api/*` is the API, everything else is the static app, so the address doesn't change and nothing new can be blocked. Data is in **D1** (`salesgo`, binding `DB`, shared by `mandoub-mestk` and `mandoub-trial`), tables in `worker/schema.sql`: `users` (email, PBKDF2-SHA256 10k rounds — kept low for the free CPU budget —, per-user `seq`, lock for 15 min after 8 wrong passwords, `off`), `sessions` (sha-256 of the token), `records (user_id, kind, id, data JSON, deleted, rev, sv)`. `POST /api/login`, `POST /api/signup`, `POST /api/sync {since, changes}` (upsert with `WHERE excluded.rev >= records.rev`, all rows of one call get the bumped `seq` in one batch, then returns rows with `sv > since`, 400 a page, never splitting a seq), `GET /api/me`, `POST /api/logout`. `sw.js` never touches `/api/`.
- **Accounts (v44: open sign-up, Hassan's choice — the rep makes his own account):** Settings → «حسابك» → «معندكش حساب؟ اعمل حساب جديد» (`accMode="up"`): name, email, password (≥6) twice → `POST /api/signup` (409 if the email exists, at most 5 new accounts per IP per day via the `signups` table) → signed in and synced like a login. **Forgotten password (v48), no email needed:** every account has a **recovery code** (`XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` from an unambiguous alphabet, stored as a PBKDF2 hash in the `recovery` table): `POST /api/signup` returns it and the app shows it once (`recoverySheet`: «ابعته لنفسي واتساب» / «انسخه» / «حفظته ✓»). «نسيت الباسورد؟» on the login form (`accMode="reset"`) → email + code + new password twice → `POST /api/reset`: wrong codes count toward the same 15-min lock as passwords; on success every other session is signed out, the code is spent and a new one comes back (shown again).
